# Visual, graphic and image design

## Creative target

Prioritize strong art direction and brand recognition. The default LeTran aesthetic, when no other brief overrides it, is:
- contemporary European;
- modern and youthful;
- fresh rather than trendy-for-trend's-sake;
- simple but luxurious;
- refined whitespace and hierarchy;
- confident product focus;
- distinctive, repeatable brand cues.

## Poster / key visual

A strong poster/KV should define:
- focal subject;
- composition and spatial logic;
- typography hierarchy;
- brand/logo zone;
- palette and contrast;
- image treatment;
- headline relationship;
- adaptation logic for other ratios.

Avoid making every concept a centered product on a generic gradient. Distinguish directions through composition architecture, scale, crop, typography, spatial treatment, photography/3D language, and brand device.

## Brand recognition

Strengthen recognition through a small repeatable system rather than simply making the logo larger. Possible devices:
- signature framing/crop;
- consistent type hierarchy;
- recurring grid or geometry;
- brand color behavior;
- distinctive product lighting;
- graphic motif derived from brand/product form;
- controlled logo placement;
- recognizable image treatment.

## Image creation and editing

When asked to create a visual, generate it when tool support exists. When asked to edit a supplied image, edit the actual image instead of merely describing steps.

For edits, preserve:
- product geometry unless asked to change it;
- logo/brand marks when supplied;
- material appearance where product accuracy matters;
- perspective and shadows plausibly.

## 2D / 3D marketing imagery

`2D` may include graphic composition, collage, typography, retouching, illustration, poster layout, social layouts, or catalog spreads.

`3D marketing visual` may include conceptual product scenes, spatial campaign environments, stylized 3D compositions, and product staging. Treat it as a marketing visual unless controlled 3D source geometry exists.

Do not imply a generated marketing image is an engineering model or CAD source.

## Variants

For a broad creative request, 2-3 strong directions are better than many weak variations. Once the user selects a direction, create channel/ratio variants while preserving the same Big Idea and recognition system.
