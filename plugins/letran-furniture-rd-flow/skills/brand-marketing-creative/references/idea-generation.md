# Strong idea generation

## Objective

Create campaign ideas that are ownable, visual, memorable and executable. Avoid mood-only directions.

## Idea anatomy

For every serious concept, define:
1. **Human / brand tension** — the contradiction, desire, problem or point of view that makes the idea interesting.
2. **Big Idea** — one sentence describing the creative proposition, not an adjective stack.
3. **Creative mechanic** — the repeatable device that makes the idea visible or experiential.
4. **Hero execution** — exactly what the main poster/KV looks like.
5. **Brand device** — the recurring element that makes the work recognizable without relying only on the logo.
6. **Copy territory** — the headline logic or verbal pattern that belongs to this direction.
7. **Channel expansion** — how the mechanic adapts to social, motion, catalog, OOH, digital, 2D or 3D.

## Strength test

Reject and regenerate a direction if two or more are true:
- it is mostly adjectives such as premium / elegant / fresh / modern;
- the poster is just a centered product on a background;
- the same idea could advertise perfume, hotel, fashion or furniture with only a logo swap;
- there is no memorable visual mechanic;
- there is no connection between visual and copy;
- the concept relies on trend styling but has no brand point of view;
- all proposed directions share the same composition architecture.

## Divergence rule

For 3 concept directions, force three different idea architectures, for example:
- **Object transformation**: product/material becomes or creates something unexpected.
- **Spatial world**: build a distinctive 2D/3D environment around one brand rule.
- **Editorial system**: use crop, type, grid, sequence and image behavior as the idea.

These are examples, not mandatory categories. The point is structural difference.

## Poster concept output

For each direction, specify:
- Name
- Big Idea
- What the viewer sees in the first 2 seconds
- Composition architecture
- Product scale/crop
- Background/world
- Typography behavior
- Signature brand device
- Headline territory
- 2D/3D/image-treatment approach
- Why it is hard to confuse with a generic competitor ad

## Creative escalation

If the user says `ý tưởng chưa đủ mạnh`, do not merely intensify adjectives. Change the idea architecture. Increase novelty through juxtaposition, scale, transformation, negative space, framing, narrative, material behavior, unexpected environment, typographic intervention, or brand-owned geometry while preserving clarity and product fidelity.
