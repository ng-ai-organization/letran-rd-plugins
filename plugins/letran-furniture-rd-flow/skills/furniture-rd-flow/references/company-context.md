# LeTran production and design context

- Products: tables, chairs, shelving, decor and custom collection products.
- Core capability: steel frames, powder coating, synthetic rattan / PE rope / webbing weaving. Cushions and complementary wood, glass, stone-look or compact materials can be proposed when appropriate.
- Target: export furniture with a modern European feel: youthful, clean, visually light, restrained but refined, harmonious proportions and manufacturable details.
- For LUNA-like chairs, the factory-confirmed baseline is separately fabricated steel frame subassemblies welded together in a final jig.
- Typical visual output: white-background JPG. Engineering exchange: PDF, DXF/DWG; rough 3D exchange can use OBJ/SCAD where feasible. Company stack: 3ds Max, SolidWorks, AutoCAD, SketchUp, AI tools, Photoshop and InDesign.
- Factory tube sizes, wall thickness, PE-rattan profile/size, bend radii and production tolerances remain project-specific/TBD unless confirmed.
