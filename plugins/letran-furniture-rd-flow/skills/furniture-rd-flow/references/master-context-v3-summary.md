# Master Context V3.0 - operational summary

## Design DNA
Modern European; young contemporary; simple but luxurious; clean geometry; soft radius/refined transitions; visually light and balanced; warm/natural material expression; no decoration without aesthetic or technical purpose.

## Internet research
When internal data is missing, research multi-brand benchmarks rather than copying a single product. Record source/date/market/price/dimensions/material/construction/KD or stackability/finish/design notes where available. Separate sourced facts from AI interpretation.

## Collection model
Collection-level: style, market, palette, powder coat family, weaving language, form/detail language.
Product-level: dimensions, ergonomics, structure, BOM, packaging, loading and product-specific risks.
Common components: use when sensible for production and purchasing.
Revision history: keep per collection and per product.

## Simplified user-facing workflow
01 Yêu cầu: brief ngắn, multi-product/custom product, optional one weaving image.
02 Tạo ý tưởng: Design DNA + three directions + concept visuals. Multi-product collections also require a coordinated lifestyle scene in the selected Indoor/Outdoor context.
03 Xem & chỉnh sửa: review collection first, then products; accumulate comments and create controlled revision.
04 Thông số kỹ thuật: dimensions/frame/weaving/cushion/construction; advanced fields hidden unless needed.
05 BOM: phân rã assembly/subassembly/part, vật tư, qty, size/cut length, process và status; không tính giá thành khi không có dữ liệu costing kiểm soát.
06 Đóng gói & Loading: screening until carton/stack/weight inputs are validated.
07 Output / R&D Package: chỉ tạo JPG/PDF/DOCX/XLSX/DXF/DWG/OBJ/SCAD/STEP khi dữ liệu và tool thực sự hỗ trợ; báo Ready/Preliminary/Not ready theo từng loại file.

## Status rules
Benchmark; AI Proposal; Calculated; Factory Confirmed; R&D Confirmed; TBD. No AI Proposal or render-derived number may silently become released production data.

## Weaving
Weaving is both visual and technical. Concept stage may define visible pattern language; engineering later defines path, profile, pitch, anchors, termination, consumption, labor and QC. One reference image is enough for visual-language input; if absent, AI proposes the weaving language.

## Review discipline
AI image generation is for geometry/visual communication. Controlled part numbers, leader lines, dimensions and BOM links must be created from controlled engineering data, not trusted from generated text embedded in images.
